Below is a summary of what we need from a field service portal for our technicians and dispatchers.

- Users sign in with their company account and see only what their role allows.
- Administrators add and remove users and assign roles.
- Dispatchers create work orders with a customer, address, priority and due date.
- Work orders move through states: new, scheduled, in progress, done, cancelled.
- Dispatchers assign work orders to technicians on a calendar.
- The calendar shows each technician's availability and travel time between jobs.
- Technicians see their day's jobs on a phone, offline if needed.
- Technicians record notes, photos and the customer's signature on completion.
- Customers receive an SMS when a technician is on the way.
- Work orders are created automatically from our CRM tickets.
- Parts used on a job are deducted from the van stock.
- Managers see dashboards for jobs completed, first-time fix rate and overdue work.
- Completed jobs are sent to billing with labour time and parts.
